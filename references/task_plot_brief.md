# Task Plot Brief

Title: Hick–Hyman Choice Reaction Task
Construct: response selection / information processing

## Rows / Conditions

- 2 choices · 1 bit: two centered outline circles, F/J mapping, one black filled target.
- 4 choices · 2 bits: four centered outline circles, D/F/J/K mapping, one black filled target.
- 8 choices · 3 bits: eight outline circles across the screen, A/S/D/F/J/K/L/; mapping, one black filled target.

## Participant-Visible Timeline

- Every row starts with a central black `+` fixation for 500 ms.
- The choice screen shows only the active outlined circles and one black filled target circle. Key labels are not shown during the response window. The screen lasts until a keypress, maximum 2000 ms.
- A compact optional error branch may be annotated below the arrow: red Chinese-free symbolic label `error / timeout` for 500 ms. Do not present it as a mandatory phase.
- Every row ends with the central black `+` ITI for 700 ms.

## Visual Accuracy Notes

- White or very light gray participant-screen interiors.
- 2-choice circles are the two innermost positions; 4-choice circles are the central four; 8-choice circles span the same total width.
- Circle sizes are identical across rows; do not enlarge 2-choice stimuli relative to 8-choice stimuli.
- Each target dot is centered inside its outline circle.
- Keep all screens at the same aspect ratio and scale.
- Use exact row labels: `2 choices · 1 bit`, `4 choices · 2 bits`, `8 choices · 3 bits`.
- Use exact phase labels: `Fixation`, `Choice response`, `ITI`.
- Use exact timing labels: `500 ms`, `2000 ms max`, `700 ms`.
- Do not draw a mouse, cursor, people, hands, keyboard, lab equipment, title, subtitle, logo, or watermark.
